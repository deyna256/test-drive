# Discount after viewing the notice

## Purpose

Check the discount after viewing the shop's test notice.

## Initial state

Open the shop in a fresh session with an empty cart.

## Steps

1. Open the Test notice and read its message.
2. Search for `mug` and add Ceramic Mug to the cart.
3. Apply the `SAVE10` discount code.

## Acceptance criteria

- The Test notice is visible.
- The cart total is $10.80 after the discount is applied.
