# Add a product to the cart

## Goal

Check that a customer can add a product to the cart.

## Initial state

The customer is signed in, and the cart is empty.

## Steps

1. Find “Kettle”.
2. Add it to the cart.
3. Open the cart.

## Acceptance criteria

- The cart shows “Kettle”.
- The quantity is one.
- The total is $19.90.
