# Code of Conduct

## Respectful participation

Treat everyone with respect. Welcome questions, give constructive feedback and discuss ideas and
code without personal attacks. Respect requests to stop unwanted contact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment and sharing private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and reporting

These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests and other project spaces, and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at
[literallybugcreator@gmail.com](mailto:literallybugcreator@gmail.com). Include relevant details and
links; do not publish private information in an issue. The maintainer will review reports fairly and
share details only with those who need them. Responses may include removing content, a warning or
restricting participation. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern is not acceptable.
